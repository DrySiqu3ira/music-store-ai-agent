**Customer:** I bought a guitar from your online store, but I've changed my
mind. Can I return it?

**Buddy:** Yes, absolutely! Don't worry—we'll help you with that.

For online purchases, you can request a return under the right of withdrawal
provided by Brazil's Consumer Protection Code and our store policy:

* **Time limit:** You can request a return within **7 calendar days** of
  receiving the guitar. No reason is required.
* **Product condition:** The instrument must be in its original packaging, show
  no signs of use, and include all manuals and items that came with it.
  *(Note: returns do not apply to instruments with custom adjustments or
  setups.)*
* **Return shipping:** **We'll cover the cost.** We'll provide the return
  shipping authorization.
* **Refund:** The refund is issued to the original payment method within
  **10 business days** after we receive and inspect the product.

To get started, could you share your **order number** or the **CPF number**
registered with the purchase? I can look up the details and explain the next
steps.
